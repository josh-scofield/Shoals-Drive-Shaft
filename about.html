Shoals Drive Shaft has built and serviced driveline components since 1996, working out of Muscle Shoals, Alabama. What started as a local driveshaft shop in the Shoals area has grown into a full driveline operation, supplying customers and fleets across the country — from family farms and independent garages to industrial plants, trucking outfits, and custom builders.

The work is straightforward: build it right, build it once. Every shaft we put together gets balanced and inspected before it leaves the shop, and every part we sell is sourced from suppliers we’ve worked with for decades. We don’t cut corners on driveline work because the people running these parts — the farmer pulling a hay rake at midnight, the truck driver heading up a mountain pass, the plant manager who can’t afford downtime — need them to hold up.

What We Do

We supply, repair, and custom-build driveshafts and powertrain components across the major application categories. If it spins between an engine and the wheels, the implement, or the load — we work on it.

Industrial Pumps, conveyors, mills, mixers, shop machinery.
Agricultural PTO and CV shafts for tractors, balers, mowers, implements.
Heavy Diesel Class 7–8 trucks, dump trucks, commercial fleets.
Passenger Cars, light trucks, SUVs, OE-style and performance.
Custom Builds Lifted trucks, race builds, retrofits, hot rods, one-offs — if it doesn’t come from a catalog, we build it.

How We Work

Most of our business comes from repeat customers and referrals, and we like it that way. It means we get to know our customers’ equipment, their applications, and their work — and we get to keep doing what we do best.
